## Staging / independence

- `M07-L01-A01-part1.md` and `part2.md` remain staged views of the same approved task.
- New information must not appear before part1 is completed and applied.
- Step4 verifies completion only; it must not prescribe a second application.
- A02 is support, not an ordered recipe.
- Early part2 viewing may reduce rehearsal quality but does not create a new certification branch; M07-L02 is the actual F1.

## Evidence

M07-L01-C01 is a rehearsal journal, not formal certification. Required observable elements are:
- completed part1 in learner note;
- meaningful handling of changed information;
- explanation of key decisions;
- verification evidence only if verification was genuinely needed;
- no ritual use of irrelevant tools.

**Quiz:** cannot replace integrated practice.
**Stepik uploads:** not required.
**F1:** explicitly not awarded here.
